We store as little as possible

SkillTube does not store IP addresses or run analytics; country stays private and minimal records expire.

2026-11-24
privacydata

Privacy should be part of a product's structure, not only a paragraph in a policy. SkillTube does not store IP addresses in its database, does not run analytics or advertising trackers, and keeps the selected country private. Country is used for aggregate counts and is not included in public developer pages or listing cards.

The service does need account information to work. GitHub numeric identity, username, display name and avatar support sign-in and public profiles. The service also stores listings, follows, stars, comments, reports, badges and counters. These records support the directory's features; they are described in the Privacy Policy so users can understand what the app retains.

Login sessions live in browser local storage as a technical part of the authentication flow. That means a person using a shared device should sign out and clear browser data when appropriate. SkillTube does not set advertising or analytics cookies, but hosting providers may process network requests to deliver the service.

When a user deletes an account, their profile, listings and community records are removed through the product's deletion flow. A minimal removal or legal record may remain for 180 days before the cleanup job purges it. Reports and comments follow their own retention rules. These limits are important because data should not be kept without a product or legal reason.

Access to database tables is not granted directly to the browser. The app calls controlled functions, and administrator permissions are checked on the server. Image uploads are validated before storage. These measures reduce exposure, but no online service can promise perfect security. The security policy explains how to report a vulnerability privately.

If you want to understand the full data flow, read the Privacy, Cookies and Security pages and inspect the open-source repository. If something is unclear, contact the team. A small data footprint is useful only when the rules and user controls are understandable.

Privacy also depends on using the product thoughtfully. Do not include private information in a public bio, listing description or comment, and sign out on a device you do not control. The service cannot control what a third-party repository owner publishes on GitHub, so review that project's own privacy practices. SkillTube's policies describe this directory's handling, not every external service.
