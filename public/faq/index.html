Basics

Is SkillTube only for AI skills?

Skills are our main focus, but you can also list MCP servers, AI-related datasets and databases, prompts, agents and AI tools.

GitHub already lets me publish and search. Why SkillTube?

SkillTube adds structured cards, 39 tags, a developer channel, fair ranking, owner verification, and clean pages that people and AI crawlers can read fully.

Can AI assistants and crawlers read SkillTube?

Yes. Public pages need no JavaScript, have Markdown twins, structured data, a sitemap and llms.txt. Source links are never placed on public pages.

Do I have to move my repository?

No. Your repository stays on GitHub; SkillTube only lists it.

What is SkillTube?

A free directory where developers list AI skills, MCP servers, datasets, databases, prompts, agents and other AI tools. Each listing links to a public GitHub repository.

Is it really free?

Yes. No payment, no subscription, no ads.

Who runs it?

Let's Code India, an open-source organisation in India.

Do you host my files?

No. Files stay in your GitHub repository.

Which languages is the site in?

English for now.

Accounts and age

Who can sign up?

Anyone aged 18 or over with a GitHub account at least 3 months old.

Why GitHub only?

It lets us verify that you own the repository you list.

Can I use it without an account?

You can browse public pages and developer profiles. Opening Source, starring, following and publishing need a login.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Delete account. Your profile, skills, follows, stars and comments are removed immediately.

Why was my account blocked or removed?

Under-age, repeated serious violations (3 badge-removal removals), or a legal order. Contact us on any of the three emails.

Publishing

How many skills can I publish?

You start with 25. More slots unlock with views, followers and invites.

How long can names and descriptions be?

Name 15 characters, short description 150, long description 1500.

What are the 39 tags?

You pick 3 topics from 15, then 9 and 27 more tags under them. All are required.

Why can't I edit right now?

Edits are rate-limited (lock shown with days left).

What image sizes are allowed?

3 KB normally, 10 KB for Blue/Master Tick holders; SVG, and PNG where the rules allow.

What happens if I delete my GitHub repo?

The listing is removed after two consecutive failed checks (about 12 hours).

Can I list someone else's repo?

No. The repo owner must be the logged-in account.

Safety and reports

How do I report a listing?

Use Report on the skill or channel; pick a reason and add a note up to 150 characters.

Does the developer see who reported?

No.

How long does the developer have to reply?

A set window; one reminder is sent three days before it ends.

What is hidden immediately?

Reports marked serious (malware, illegal, violence/terror) hide the listing at once.

Copyright complaint?

Use the `takedown_request` issue template or email us.

Badges and ticks

How do I earn a badge?

Each badge page shows "how to earn".

What is the Blue Tick?

1,000,000 followers. It means fame, not identity verification.

What is the Master Tick?

100 Blue-Tick followers from at least 10 countries.

What are the weekly ranks?

The top 50 developers by weekly score in bands of ten; only the best band shows on your profile.

Are badges removed?

Yes, if a skill that earned a badge is removed after a report.

Privacy

What do you store?

GitHub id, username, avatar URL, the country you choose, follows, stars, comments, reports, counters and badges.

Do you store my IP?

Never.

Do you use analytics or tracking cookies?

No.

Is my country public?

Never.

How long do you keep removal records?

180 days.

Who handles complaints?

Our Grievance Officer — see the Grievance page.

Technical

Is the code open?

Yes, MIT.

How do I report a security bug?

See the Security page and `.well-known/security.txt`.

Does it work offline?

Only a basic offline page.

Is there an API?

Not public yet.

Where can I see service status?

The Status page.

Where is the documentation?

Read the SkillTube documentation for guides to publishing, limits, reports, privacy and AI-tool access.
