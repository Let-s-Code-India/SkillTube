Cookies and Local Storage

Last updated: 24 November 2026

SkillTube sets no advertising, analytics or tracking cookies and shows no cookie banner. To keep you signed in, the app stores your login session in your browser's local storage (a technical necessity). The service worker caches only static files (never your data). You can sign out or clear site data in your browser at any time. Our hosting provider may set short-lived technical cookies or headers needed for security and delivery; they are not used to track you.
