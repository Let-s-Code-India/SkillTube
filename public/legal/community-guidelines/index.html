Community Guidelines

Last updated: 24 November 2026

SkillTube works when people trust it. Be useful, be honest, be kind.

Do: list only your own public repositories; describe them truthfully; credit original authors and forks; pick accurate tags; answer reports politely; disclose if a resource collects data, needs payment elsewhere, or runs code on the user's machine.

Don't:

  • List malware, ransomware, spyware, stealers, credential harvesters or tools whose main purpose is to attack systems without consent.
  • Post illegal content, content that promotes violence or terrorism, hate, harassment, or sexual content involving minors (reported to authorities).
  • Post datasets containing personal data without a lawful basis, leaked databases, or scraped private data.
  • Impersonate people or projects, or mislead with fake names, tags or images.
  • Spam, keyword-stuff tags, fake views/stars/follows, or run multiple accounts.
  • Post comments that abuse, threaten, doxx or advertise.
  • Misuse badges, ticks or reports.

Consequences: warning, hiding a listing, removal, badge removal, and account block. Serious reports hide a listing immediately while we review. Three removals of badge-earning skills block the account. You can reply to any report in the app or write to us.
