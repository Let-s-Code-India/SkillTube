Copyright and Takedown Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

We respect intellectual property and follow the Copyright Act, 1957 and the Information Technology Act, 2000 with its Rules. SkillTube stores only listing text and a link, not the files.

To report infringement, open an issue with the takedown_request template at https://github.com/Let-s-Code-India/SkillTube/issues/new/choose or email any of the three contact emails with: (1) your name and contact details; (2) the work you own or represent and where it is; (3) the SkillTube URL; (4) why it infringes; (5) a statement that your information is accurate and you are the owner or authorised; (6) your signature (typed is fine).

What happens: we acknowledge within 72 hours, notify the developer, and, if the claim looks valid, hide or remove the listing. The developer may reply (for example fixed, removed, disagree, credited the fork, or false report). Repeated false claims may lead to restrictions. Repeat infringers are blocked.
