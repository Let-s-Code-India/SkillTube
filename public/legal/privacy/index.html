Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

1. Who we are. Let's Code India operates SkillTube, a free directory of AI skills, MCP servers, datasets, databases, prompts, agents and other AI tools. For the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act") we are the Data Fiduciary. Contact: the three emails above.

Grievance Officer: Ayush Giri, Founder, Let's Code India, India. Contact: ayushgiri.ai.21@gmail.com, ayushgiri.25@icloud.com or ayushgiri.dev1@outlook.com.

2. Who may use SkillTube. SkillTube is only for people aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly process data of children. If we learn an account belongs to a person under 18, we delete it.

3. What we collect and why.

  • From GitHub when you sign in: your numeric GitHub id, username, display name, avatar URL, and account creation date. Purpose: to create your account, show your public channel, and check that you own the repositories you list and that your GitHub account is old enough.
  • Chosen by you: the country you select (private, never shown to anyone, used only for aggregate per-country counts), profile text and image, interests.
  • Your activity: skills you publish, follows, stars, comments, reports you file, invite codes, badges earned, and counters such as views and source opens.
  • Technical: your login session token, kept in your browser's local storage. Our hosting provider and database provider process requests to run the Service; we do not store IP addresses and we do not run analytics or advertising trackers.
  • We do not collect payments, government identity numbers, precise location, contacts, or any sensitive personal data.

4. Legal basis. Under the DPDP Act we process your data with your consent, given when you sign in and confirm you are 18 or over and accept these documents, and for legitimate uses such as complying with law and responding to legal notices. You may withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account.

5. What is public. Your username, avatar, bio, skills you list (name, short description, tags, counters, badges) and follower count are public. Your country, who reported you, and your activity feed are not public. Public pages may be indexed by search engines and AI crawlers; Source links are available only inside the signed-in app.

6. Sharing. We do not sell or rent data. Processors we use, all with free plans: Supabase (database, authentication, storage), Cloudflare (hosting and delivery), GitHub (sign-in, code hosting, automation). They process data only to provide those services and may store it outside India. We may disclose data when required by law or a competent authority.

7. Retention. Account data is kept while your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, skills, follows, stars and comments are deleted at once. We keep a minimal removal or legal record (your GitHub id, what was removed, and why) for 180 days and then delete it automatically. Comments expire on their own schedule shown in the product. Reports are kept only as long as needed to resolve them and for the same 180-day limit.

8. Security. Tables are closed to the public internet; the website can only call controlled functions; admin rights are checked on the server; images are sanitised; secrets never reach the browser. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects you we will notify you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

9. Your rights (DPDP Act). You may access a summary of your data, correct it, erase it, nominate another person to exercise your rights in case of death or incapacity, and raise a grievance. Most of this is in Settings; for anything else, write to us. We reply within 30 days.

10. Grievance. See the Grievance page. If we do not resolve your complaint you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

11. Cookies. See the Cookies page.

12. Changes. If we change this policy in a material way we will update the date and show a notice in the app.
