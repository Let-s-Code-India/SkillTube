Badges and ranking

Badges and ticks are automatic activity signals displayed on SkillTube. They are not identity verification, and they appear only on SkillTube.

Last updated: 2026-11-25

On this page

  1. Ticks
  2. Weekly ranking
  3. Badge removal
  4. Need help?

Ticks

The Blue Tick is earned at 1,000 followers. It represents fame, not identity verification.

The Master Tick is earned when a developer has 100 Blue-Tick followers from at least 10 countries. It does not mean that SkillTube has verified a person's legal identity.

Admin settings override these static fallback values at runtime.

Weekly ranking

The weekly ranking shows the top 50 developers in bands of ten. A developer's profile shows their best band. To qualify, the developer's first listing must be at least 3 months old.

Badge removal

If a listing that earned a badge is removed after a report, the badge is revoked. See the badge catalog for current badges and earning rules.

Need help?

For questions about an earned or revoked badge, contact us.
