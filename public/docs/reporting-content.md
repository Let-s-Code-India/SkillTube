# Reporting content

Report a listing or developer channel when you believe it breaks the community rules. Reports are reviewed, and a report is not by itself proof that a claim is true.

## How to report

1. Open the listing or developer channel in the app.
2. Choose **Report**, select the reason that best fits, and add an optional note of up to 150 characters.
3. Submit the report once. You can report a target only once per 24 hours and file up to 10 reports per 24 hours.

The seven reasons are **malware**, **hacking tool**, **illegal**, **violence/terror**, **copyright**, **spam/fake**, and **other**.

## What happens next

The developer is told what was reported, but is not shown who reported it. Serious listing reasons—malware, hacking tool, illegal, and violence/terror—hide the listing immediately while it is reviewed. Copyright, spam/fake, and other reports do not hide a listing immediately. Either side could be wrong, so the review and response process matters.

The developer has 10 days to answer a report. A reminder is sent when 3 days remain. The team reviews cases that need its decision and may remove a listing, block an account, or dismiss the report and return the listing.

False or abusive reports can lead to account restrictions. A copyright claim can also be submitted through the [Copyright and Takedown page](/legal/copyright/) using the GitHub `takedown_request` issue form.
