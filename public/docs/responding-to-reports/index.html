Responding to reports

If someone reports your listing or channel, SkillTube gives you a private notice and a response window. You can see what was reported, but never who submitted the report.

Last updated: 2026-11-25

On this page

  1. Find the report and respond in time
  2. Choose an answer
  3. Waiting times
  4. Make a useful response
  5. Need help?

Find the report and respond in time

Check the app's Alerts and the red notification dot. Open the notice to see the target, reason and any note. You have 10 days to answer. SkillTube sends one reminder when 3 days remain.

You may add a message of up to 1,500 characters. Explain the facts and include evidence that helps the team understand the change.

Choose an answer

Your answer Effect
I fixed it Closes an ordinary case. A serious case still goes to an administrator.
Credit given to the fork/original Closes an ordinary case. A serious case still goes to an administrator.
I removed it Removes the listing and revokes any badge it earned.
I disagree Keeps the case open for the team to review.
I need more information Keeps the case open for the team to review.
This report is false Sends the claim to the team for review.

The team decides whether to remove the listing, block the account, or dismiss the report. If dismissed, a hidden listing returns.

Waiting times

Step Time
Your response window 10 days from the notice
Reminder One reminder with 3 days left
Appeal acknowledgement Within 72 hours
Appeal resolution target Within 30 days

If you do not answer in time, a serious listing report removes the listing, an ordinary listing report expires, and a channel report deletes the account.

Make a useful response

  • Say what you changed and when.
  • Link to a relevant commit if you fixed the repository.
  • Explain the licence or provide evidence that you have permission.
  • If credit is relevant, show where you added it.
  • Keep your message factual and within 1,500 characters.

Need help?

For a decision appeal, follow Removals and appeals. For other questions, contact us.
