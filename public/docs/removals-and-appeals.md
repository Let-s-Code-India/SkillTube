# Removals and appeals

This guide explains what happens when a listing or account is removed and how to ask the team to review a decision.

## When a listing is removed

The listing and its images are removed, its counters are cleared, and a badge earned from that listing is revoked. A minimal removal record is kept for 180 days, then deleted.

Three removals of listings that earned badges lead to an account block.

## When an account is blocked

All of the account's listings are removed. The GitHub account used for that account cannot sign up again. Administrative decisions are logged.

## Deleting your own content

You can delete your own listing from **My listings**. You can undo that deletion within 12 hours. After that window, it cannot be restored through the app.

You can delete your account in **Settings**. The account's profile, listings, follows, stars and comments are removed. A minimal removal or legal record may remain for 180 days as described in the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy/).

## Appeal a decision

Email us with the subject **Appeal**. Include:

1. Your GitHub username.
2. The listing URL.
3. The report date.
4. What you changed or why you believe the decision should be reviewed.

We acknowledge an appeal within 72 hours and aim to resolve it within 30 days. The team reviews the record and decides whether to uphold or change the outcome.
