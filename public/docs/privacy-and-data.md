# Privacy and data

SkillTube stores account and activity information needed to run the service. Some profile and listing information is public; private account details are not.

## What is stored

When you use SkillTube, the service processes your GitHub account details, chosen country, profile information, listings, follows, stars, comments, reports, invites, badges and activity counters. Hosting and service providers also process requests needed to deliver the site.

SkillTube does not store IP addresses or run analytics and advertising trackers. Your chosen country and the identity of a reporter are not public.

## What is public

Your username, avatar, bio, listings, listing descriptions and tags, public activity counts, badges and follower count can be seen on public pages and indexed by search engines or AI crawlers. Source links are available only in the signed-in app. Country, reporter identity, private account information and the private activity feed are not shown publicly.

## Retention and deletion

Account information is kept while your account exists. Deleting your account removes your profile, listings, follows, stars and comments. A minimal removal or legal record can be kept for 180 days. Reports are retained only as needed to resolve them and within the same 180-day limit.

To delete your account, sign in, open **Settings**, and choose **Delete account**. Read the confirmation carefully before proceeding.
