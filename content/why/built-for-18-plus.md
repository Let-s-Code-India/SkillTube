SkillTube is for people aged 18 or older. The service handles public publishing, reports, licensing questions and account decisions. Requiring adult users is a product rule intended to support accountability around those activities. It is not a claim that every adult will use the service responsibly; the Community Guidelines and reporting process still apply to everyone.

Publishing also requires an eligible GitHub account that is at least three months old. GitHub sign-in lets the service check repository ownership and account age. The account-age requirement adds friction for brand-new accounts, but it helps reduce anonymous or disposable publishing abuse. Browsing public listings does not require the same sign-in flow.

At first sign-in, the app asks the user to confirm they are 18 or older and accept the Terms and Privacy Policy. The current confirmation is stored locally as a convenience flag; the backend does not yet retain a durable acceptance timestamp. The service also checks account eligibility through its account-gate function.

If the team learns that an account belongs to someone under 18, the account is not permitted and will be removed. Users should not create an account on behalf of a minor or submit a minor's personal information. The Privacy Policy explains what account data is processed and how to raise a grievance or request deletion.

These age requirements are part of the product's operating boundaries, not a guarantee that all listed resources are suitable for every adult. AI skills, MCP servers, datasets, databases, prompts, agents and tools may have risks, licences or requirements. Read their documentation and use them carefully.

If you cannot or do not want to sign in, you can still browse public pages and read the site policies. If you have an eligibility question, contact the team before attempting to publish. The rules should be clear before someone shares a project.

The age rule also applies to the material people publish. Developers should not submit content involving minors in prohibited ways or datasets containing personal information without a lawful basis. The service's reporting process can address policy violations, and serious reports may hide a listing while they are reviewed. Read the full Community Guidelines and Privacy Policy before creating an account.
