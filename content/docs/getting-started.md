# Getting started

Sign in with GitHub to publish and manage AI resources on SkillTube. AI skills are the main focus; MCP servers, datasets, databases, prompts, agents, AI tools and guides are welcome too.

Last updated: 2026-11-25

## Who can use SkillTube

SkillTube is for people aged 18 or older. Confirm your age and terms in the app, then choose a country to finish account setup. Your country is private and is not shown on your public channel.

You can browse public pages without signing in. GitHub sign-in is required to publish, manage listings, open a listing's Source button, star listings or follow developers.

## Publish your first listing

1. Sign in with GitHub and open **Publish**.
2. Choose what you are listing: an AI skill, MCP server, dataset, database, prompt pack, agent, AI tool or guide.
3. Add a name of up to 15 characters, a short description of up to 150 characters and a long description of up to 1,500 characters.
4. Choose 3 top-level topics, then 9 subtopics and 27 detail tags. All 39 tags are required.
5. Add an image that meets the image-size rule: up to 3 KB normally, or up to 10 KB for an eligible tick holder.
6. Submit the listing. SkillTube checks that the signed-in GitHub account owns the public repository.

Your listing is a discovery card and description; the project files remain in GitHub. The **Source** button opens the developer's own GitHub repository.

## Keep your listing useful

Use a specific name, explain what the resource does, choose accurate tags and make sure the repository and its licence are ready for visitors. Read the [limits and rules](/docs/limits-and-rules/) before publishing.
